## Reporting rules

- **Signed.** A partner appears here only after a signed engagement
  letter (design-partner terms) exists.
- **Redaction.** A partner may request the row be redacted; redacted
  form is what appears here.
- **Case studies.** A case study lands under
  `docs/data-room/traction/case-studies/<partner>.md` only with
  written approval.
